# Adventure Forest Android Game

Welcome to Adventure Forest Game (Inspired by Little Red Riding Hood), an exciting Android mobile game developed in Unity 3D. Explore the mystical forest, face challenging obstacles, and collect treasures on your adventure! This game combines beautiful 2D graphics, engaging gameplay, and captivating soundtracks for an immersive experience.

**Features:**
- **Engaging Gameplay:** Navigate through various levels filled with challenging obstacles and dynamic enemies.
- **Stunning 2D Graphics:** Fully designed 2D environments and characters built in Unity.
- **Multiple Levels:** Unlock new stages and environments as you progress.
- **Power-Ups and Collectibles:** Gather coins, power-ups, and special treasures to boost your performance.
- **Intuitive Controls:** Designed for a smooth mobile gaming experience with touch controls.

---

#### Gameplay
Embark on a thrilling adventure through dense forests filled with hidden secrets, traps, and puzzles. Jump, run, and defeat enemies as you make your way through progressively difficult levels. Be prepared for exciting boss fights and hidden areas that challenge your gaming skills.

---

**Objectives:**
- Complete each level by reaching the goal.
- Avoid traps, obstacles, and defeat enemies along the way.
- Collect treasures, coins, and power-ups to boost your score.

---

#### Controls
- Swipe Left/Right: Move the player left or right.
- Tap to Jump: Tap the screen to make your character jump over obstacles.
- Hold to Glide: Tap the jump button and move the player forward or backward to glide for a longer time.
- Power Ups: Collide on the special items along the way when they appear to gain specific Ups (e.g., Health, Coins).

---

#### Development
This game was developed using Unity 3D, a cross-platform game engine, and built specifically for Android devices.

**Technologies Used:**
- **Unity 3D 2020.1.11f1:** Game development engine for creating 3D environments and gameplay mechanics.
- **C#:** Scripting language used for gameplay logic, AI, and controls.
- **Android SDK:** For building and deploying the game on Android devices.
- **Photoshop:** 2D Sprite modeling tool for character, environment and UI design.
